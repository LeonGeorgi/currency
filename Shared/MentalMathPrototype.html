What the search considers

Only doubling, halving, ×3, ×5, decimal shifts and percentage corrections built from halves and tenths. No eighths, sixths, division by 3–9, or 12.5% shortcuts. At most one percentage correction. The default cap is four elementary actions, including decimal shifts. A 25% correction counts as two halvings plus addition/subtraction; a 15% correction has four actions and three retained values. Compact labels do not count as single effortless operations.

The default targets 2% error and may accept up to 3% when it saves an elementary action. No independently simple rule is returned when that limit cannot be met with the conservative vocabulary and work budget. Every found rule also gets its exact inverse: reverse the step order and undo each operation. Inverses are shown even outside that vocabulary or error limit, without assigning an uncalibrated difficulty score. Adding 10% does not undo subtracting 10%; multiplying by 10 and then dividing by 9 does. The inverse undoes the approximate rule, not the market conversion, so its market error is calculated separately. Percentages apply to the current result. Do not round between steps.

The vocabulary and work counts are engineering choices informed by arithmetic research, not measured human response times. Actual difficulty still depends on the amount, carries, practice and individual skill. Read the primary research note for studies and limits.
