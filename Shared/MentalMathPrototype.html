Mental currency rules

Throwaway logic prototype. Can a short search find currency conversion rules that are both easy to remember and accurate enough? Explore the trade-off with fixed Frankfurter reference rates. This is an algorithm experiment, not a change to the iOS app.

Guided examples

What the search considers

Up to three mental operations, plus one decimal shift: multiply or divide by integers 2–9, or add/subtract 5%, 10%, 12.5%, 20%, 25%, 50%, one third, or one sixth. At most one percentage adjustment per rule. The default targets 2% error and tries two operations before allowing a third. It accepts up to 3% when the stricter rule costs over 0.8 extra effort points, to avoid a large jump in difficulty for a tiny accuracy gain. A fixed effort heuristic penalizes harder arithmetic. Within the chosen error bound, the least expensive rule wins; precision breaks ties. “More accurate” uses a 1% limit.

Every percentage or fractional adjustment applies to the result of the previous step. For example, adding 12.5% means dividing that result by 8 and adding that share. Avoid rounding between steps. Reverse conversions are searched independently. Error percentages assume arithmetic without intermediate rounding. The effort weights are a design hypothesis, not validated measurements of human performance.
